# Gold (budget 1.2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Gold, a small pile of rough cast gold: two or three small hand-poured ingots with uneven tops and a few nuggets, ~0.20 x 0.13 x 0.06 m. Warm deep-yellow metal, very low roughness on high points, slightly duller in pits and casting flaws, a faint dirty film in crevices.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
