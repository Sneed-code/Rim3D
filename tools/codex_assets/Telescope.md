# Telescope (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Telescope, a recreation building colonists use to stargaze, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~1.7 m tall. A
sturdy wooden or steel tripod with spreaders, an equatorial mount with counterweight bar and weight, a
~1 m long white refractor tube with a dew shield, a finder scope, a focuser with a knob and an eyepiece at
the back, a small accessory tray on the tripod. Worn paint, brass fittings with patina, scratches.
Separate parts: `Base`, `Tube` (pivot at the mount head). Empty `Eyepiece`.
Axis: building convention, the eyepiece end towards -Y (where the colonist stands), the tube tilted up to +Y.
