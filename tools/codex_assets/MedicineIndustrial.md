# MedicineIndustrial (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: MedicineIndustrial, a stack of industrial medicine lying on the ground, ~0.30 x 0.20 x 0.15 m.

green plus in a circle, not a red cross), with a roll of bandage, a blister strip of pills and a small
glass vial lying against it. Scuffed plastic, a dirty handprint, a split corner taped.

Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
