# PsychiteTea (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Psychite tea: a stoppered glass bottle of dark amber herbal tea brewed from psychoid leaves, ~0.08 x 0.08 x 0.22 m, standing upright. Thick reused glass, a carved wooden cork stopper sealed with wax, a hand-tied twine collar and a few dried green leaves tucked under it. Liquid modelled as a separate inner volume (dark amber, low roughness) inside a mostly clear glass shell (use the BaseColor alpha / glass-like roughness in one material slot, or a second material for glass).
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
