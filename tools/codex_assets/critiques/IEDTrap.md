# Art director critique, round 2 (improve the existing asset in this folder; same deliverables as BRIEF.md)
The current trap does not read as a bomb. Fix:
- The shells look like orange-camouflage cones. Make two real artillery shells: cylindrical body with a pointed ogive nose and fuze, a copper driving band near the base, a flat base; olive drab paint mostly rusted, not camouflage. ~0.15 m x 0.55 m each, lying side by side and half sunk into the earth (cut the earth around them, a bit of soil on top).
- The wiring is missing. Add clearly visible coloured wires (red, black, yellow), taped to the shells' fuzes with black electrical tape, running to a battery pack wrapped in tape and to the pressure plate.
- The two boards are just lying there. Make a crude pressure plate: two boards stacked with a small gap, a tin-can or foil contact between them, wires going in.
- Make the earth patch smaller and less like a pancake: about 0.9 x 0.9 m, edges fading into a thin irregular outline, darker damp red tropical soil with leaf litter (the game ground will be around it).
- Keep under 6k tris, keep `Body` and `BlastCenter`. Re-render all previews and the contact sheet, update README.md.
