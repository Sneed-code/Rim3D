# Art director critique, round 2 (improve the existing asset in this folder; same deliverables as BRIEF.md)
The current launcher reads as a toy blaster, not a real weapon. Fix:
- It has two pistol grips of the same size; make one real pistol grip with a trigger guard, and replace the front one with a slim vertical foregrip or a ribbed barrel shroud. Hands must clearly know where to go.
- The barrel is short, fat and capped like a flashlight. Make a proper 40 mm rifled steel barrel ~30-35 cm long with a plain crowned muzzle (rifling visible inside), a bare parkerised steel finish, and a clear break-open hinge at the breech under the receiver.
- The receiver is a thin flat plate with a sticker; give it real volume (a machined steel or polymer receiver ~6-7 cm wide), a visible break-open latch lever, and a rail on top for the ladder sight.
- Remove the big "SERVICE" tag and the round lying on top. Put the 40 mm round where the brief says: in the open chamber position, aligned with the bore.
- Overall length 0.75-0.85 m, height ~0.25 m without the sight up. Look at real break-open 40 mm launchers for proportions (don't copy any one product).
- Keep the parts and names from BRIEF.md and keep under 18k tris. Re-render all previews and the contact sheet, update README.md.
