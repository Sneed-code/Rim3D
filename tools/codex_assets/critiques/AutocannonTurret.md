# Art director critique, round 2 (improve the existing asset in this folder; same deliverables as BRIEF.md)
Good shapes, but it looks clean and toy-like. Fix:
- Weathering: much stronger. Chipped paint to bare steel on every edge and corner, rust streaks running down from bolts and seams, oil and soot streaks under and behind the gun, dust and red tropical mud on the base and lower third, sun-faded paint on top faces. Vary roughness a lot (worn edges shinier, dusty tops matte).
- The gun house's sides are big flat slabs: add armour plate seams, weld beads, bolted access panels and angled plate edges so it looks like welded armour.
- The barrel is thin for a ~30 mm autocannon: thicken it, add a proper recoil sleeve/cradle and a heavier muzzle brake with ports.
- Add an ammunition feed chute from a box magazine on the side into the gun, as the brief asked.
- Keep parts/names and under 35k tris. Re-render all previews and the contact sheet, update README.md.
