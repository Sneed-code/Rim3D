# TripleRocketLauncher (budget 22k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_TripleRocketLauncher, a heavy shoulder-fired launcher that fires three rockets one after
another. ~1.15-1.25 m long, ~7 kg. Three launch tubes in a triangular cluster (two below, one above, ~70 mm
each) held by two clamp rings, a shoulder rest and padded cheek piece, pistol grip and a forward grip
under the tubes, an optical/thermal sight box on the left side on a rail, a blast shield/venturi flare at
the rear of each tube, a cable from the grip to the tubes, warning stripes near the rear. Military green or
desert tan paint, chipped to bare metal at edges, tubes slightly sooted at the rear. Front covers removed;
the noses of three loaded rockets visible inside the front of the tubes.
Separate parts: `Body` (everything fixed), `Trigger` (pivot at its pin), `Rocket_1`, `Rocket_2`, `Rocket_3`
(each a full rocket ~70 mm x 0.6 m with fins folded, origin at the rocket's centre, sitting inside its
tube, so we can hide each one as it fires and fly it).
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on the main pistol grip at origin, rockets fly towards +X).
