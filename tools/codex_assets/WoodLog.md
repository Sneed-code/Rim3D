# WoodLog (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: wood, the basic building resource, a stack item lying on the ground (chopped from jungle trees). A small bundle of three cut tropical hardwood logs lying side by side, two below and one on top, each ~0.55 m long and 0.10-0.14 m thick, ~0.55 x 0.30 x 0.22 m overall. Axe-cut ends showing growth rings, pale sapwood and reddish-brown heartwood with radial cracks; rough fibrous bark (grey-brown, partly peeled away on one log to show the pale wood), a stub of a cut branch, a few moss flecks and damp dark patches. Bark really modelled (not just a texture on a cylinder); matte, not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre), logs lying along X.
