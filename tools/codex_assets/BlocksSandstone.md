# BlocksSandstone (budget 2.5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: sandstone blocks, a building resource, a stack item lying on the ground. A neat small stack of six to eight rough-cut rectangular sandstone blocks (each ~0.20 x 0.10 x 0.08 m, slightly irregular, rounded crumbly corners, sawn faces with fine grain), two layers crosswise plus one leaning, ~0.42 x 0.30 x 0.20 m overall. Warm ochre and rust-red with paler buff bands that differ block to block, sand grains on top. Each block really modelled with bevels, small gaps between; rough, not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
