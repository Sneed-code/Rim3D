# ChunkSlate (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: a slate rock chunk, a heavy loose boulder left by mining or a collapse, lying on the ground. ~0.62 x 0.46 x 0.26 m, flat bottom. Slate splits in thin sheets: a flattish wedge of stacked layers, the edges showing many thin leaves (some flaking off, a couple partly split apart), the broad top face smooth and slightly wavy, sharp edges. Dark blue-grey to charcoal, a faint purple-green tint in places, rusty brown iron stains along the cleavage, a few pale scratches. Top face a bit smoother (G ~0.6), edges rough; not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
