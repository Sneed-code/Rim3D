# ProstheticArm (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: prosthetic arm, a simple industrial mechanical arm prosthesis (below bionics), an item lying on the ground (not worn), ~0.72 m from the shoulder cup to the fingertips. A practical steel-tube-and-plastic arm: padded shoulder cup with harness straps, steel struts with an exposed elbow hinge and a cable-and-pulley tendon run, a three-finger-and-thumb gripper hand with rubber pads, a small battery pod on the forearm. Painted beige/olive plastic covers chipped to bare metal, grease at the joints, light rust. Lying on its side, elbow slightly bent.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, the arm along X, the hand towards +X).
