# TubeTelevision (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: TubeTelevision, an old CRT television colonists watch, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~1.0 m tall. A boxy
cathode-ray TV with a rounded glass screen in a woodgrain or beige plastic cabinet, side speaker grille,
two rotary knobs, a rabbit-ears antenna on top, standing on a low wooden stand with a shelf; a frayed cable
out the back. Dusty, sun-faded, a crack in the casing taped over. Screen faintly emissive (a soft blue-grey
glow, no picture).
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Screen`.
Axis: building convention, the screen facing -Y.
