# SunLamp (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: SunLamp, a powerful grow lamp that lights crops indoors, 1 x 1 cell, ~2.4 m tall. A heavy weighted steel
base, a telescoping pole, and a big wide reflector hood with a bank of bright grow bulbs on an adjustable yoke,
cooling fins on top, a ballast box on the pole. Worn industrial paint, scuffs.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Bulbs` (own bright emissive material).
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
