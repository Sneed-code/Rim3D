# Gun_HeavyChargeBlaster (budget 16k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: Gun_HeavyChargeBlaster, the big automatic pulse-charge gun carried by mechanoid centipedes (colonists
can pick it up, it is very heavy), ~1.5 m long, ~15 kg. Ultratech: a long thick body of gunmetal and pale
ceramic plates, three stacked barrels in a rotating housing with charge coils (pale blue emissive), a big
box power cell, a carry handle and a forward grip, heat vents with darkened fins. Precise but battle-worn,
scratches and scorch at the muzzles.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Barrels` (pivot on the barrel cluster's axis so it can spin). Empty `Muzzle` at the
barrel cluster's front centre.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on the rear grip at origin).
