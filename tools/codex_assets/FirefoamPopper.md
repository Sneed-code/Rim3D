# FirefoamPopper (budget 6k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: FirefoamPopper, a 1 x 1 m one-shot fire suppressor that bursts and covers the area in foam when it
senses fire, ~0.6 m tall. A squat round red pressure canister on a small tripod or base plate, a domed burst cap
with radial score lines on top, a heat sensor bulb, a pressure gauge, a safety pin with a tag, white warning
stencils, scuffs.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Burst` at the cap.
Axis: building convention, the gauge towards -Y.
