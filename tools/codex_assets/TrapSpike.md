# TrapSpike (budget 5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: TrapSpike, a hidden spike trap, one 1 x 1 m cell, nearly flat (~0.25 m). A shallow pit frame of
rough planks flush with the ground, a spring board on a hinge inside, rows of sharpened wooden and scrap
steel spikes, loose palm leaves and twigs scattered over half of it (part of the mesh; a second material
with alpha-cut leaves is allowed). Muddy, the spike tips stained dark.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Spikes` (pivot at the hinge, so it can spring up).
Axis: building convention, bottom at z = 0 (the ground surface at z = 0.05).
