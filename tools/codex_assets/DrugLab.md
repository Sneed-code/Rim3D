# DrugLab (budget 18k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: DrugLab, a 3 x 1 m chemistry workbench for making medicines and drugs, ~1.6 m tall. A worn
steel lab bench with glassware (flasks, condenser coil, beakers with coloured liquids as their own glass
material), a hot plate, a small fume hood at one end, a mortar and pestle, a scale, labelled jars, a
clipboard, rubber tubing, chemical stains and burn marks on the top. Frontier lab, not clean.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Glass` (all glass and liquids, two-sided transmissive materials allowed).
Axis: building convention, the working side towards -Y.
