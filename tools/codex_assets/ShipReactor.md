# ShipReactor (budget 40k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: ShipReactor, the starship's power reactor the colonists must build and defend while it starts up,
6 x 7 m footprint (6 wide along X, 7 deep along Y), ~4 m tall. A massive cylindrical fusion reactor core in
a heavy armoured cradle: a central vertical core vessel ~3 m across with thick reinforcing rings, a band of
inspection windows showing a bright cyan-white plasma glow (emissive mask), four squat coolant towers or
heat exchangers at the corners joined by thick insulated pipes, a raised maintenance walkway with railings
and a ladder, a control console with screens at the front (-Y), cable trays, warning lamps. Painted steel
plates with hazard stripes and stencils, grime streaks, heat discoloration near the core, worn walkway grip.
Should read as the most important, most expensive object in the colony.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Core` (the glowing core vessel, so it can pulse). Empty `Console` at the screen.
Axis: building convention, console side towards -Y.
