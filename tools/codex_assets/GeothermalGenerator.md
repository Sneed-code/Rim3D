# GeothermalGenerator (budget 30k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: GeothermalGenerator, a 2 x 2 m power plant built over a steam geyser (the ground under it is a
hot vent; leave the very bottom open in the middle so our steam effect can rise through a grate). ~2.5 m
tall: a heavy steel turbine housing with a cylindrical turbine casing and a flywheel/generator on top, big
insulated pipes, a pressure gauge cluster, valves with red handwheels, a steam exhaust chimney stack
venting upward, mineral crusting and rust streaks near the base, warning stripes, bolted flanges.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Flywheel` (pivot on its spin axis, can rotate), empty `SteamOut` at the chimney
top.
Axis: building convention, the gauge side towards -Y.
