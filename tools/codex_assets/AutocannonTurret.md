# AutocannonTurret (budget 35k tris, 2048 textures, may use two 2048 sets)
Game use: Turret_Autocannon, a heavy emplaced gun turret on a 2 x 2 m footprint, ~2.2 m tall. A
squat armoured octagonal or round steel base with access hatch and vents, a heavy traverse ring, an
armoured gun house with angled plates, a single long-barrelled ~30 mm autocannon with a muzzle brake
and recoil sleeve, an ammunition feed chute from a box on the side, a sensor/targeting block with two
lenses, grab handles, lifting eyes, warning stripes on the base. Battleship grey or olive, worn, oil
streaks under the gun, soot at the muzzle.
Separate parts: `Base` (fixed), `Head` (yaw pivot on the vertical axis through the origin), `Gun` (pitch
pivot at the trunnions), `Barrel` (recoil, pivot at its rear). Empty `Muzzle` at the brake.
Axis: building convention, barrel towards -Y at rest.
