# Synthread (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: synthread, a high-tech synthetic fabric, a stack item. Two industrial spools of fine synthetic thread on dark plastic reels lying beside a folded swatch of the woven fabric, ~0.28 x 0.20 x 0.10 m. Silvery off-white thread with a faint iridescent sheen, very fine tight weave on the swatch, a small generic barcode-style label block (no readable text). Clean but a little dusty.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
