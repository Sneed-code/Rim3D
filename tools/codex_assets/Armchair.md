# Armchair (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Armchair, a comfortable padded chair, 1 x 1 cell (~0.85 x 0.85 m), ~0.95 m tall. A frontier armchair with a
wooden frame and padded cushions in worn woven upholstery, rolled arms, a patched cushion, a folded throw over one
arm. Keep the wood fairly neutral (stuff tint).
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Seat` at the sitting spot.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
