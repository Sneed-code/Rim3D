# BlocksLimestone (budget 2.5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: limestone blocks, a building resource, a stack item lying on the ground. A neat small stack of six to eight rough-cut rectangular limestone blocks (each ~0.20 x 0.10 x 0.08 m, slightly irregular, chisel-dressed faces, softened chipped corners, a few small pits and a fossil imprint on one), two layers crosswise plus one leaning, ~0.42 x 0.30 x 0.20 m overall. Pale warm cream-grey, faint bedding lines, pale dust. Each block really modelled with bevels, small gaps between; rough, not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
