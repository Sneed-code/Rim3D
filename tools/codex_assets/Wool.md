# Wool (budget 2.5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: wool, sheared fleece used as cloth, a stack item lying on the ground (one look for every kind of wool; the game may tint it). A bundled raw fleece: a puffy rolled bale of crimped wool tied round the middle with plant-fibre twine, a few loose tufts on the ground beside it, ~0.34 x 0.26 x 0.20 m. Off-white to cream with faint yellowish lanolin tint and a little dust; strongly crimped, lumpy fibre clumps really modelled in the silhouette, fine fibre detail in the normal map. Matte, soft.
