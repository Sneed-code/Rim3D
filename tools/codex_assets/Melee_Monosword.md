# Melee_Monosword (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Melee_Monosword, an ultratech sword with a mono-molecular edge, ~1.0 m long overall, ~1.3 kg.
A slim straight single-edged blade (~0.72 m) of dark grey-blue alloy with a mirror-bright razor edge strip
that faintly glows pale cyan (emissive line), fine engraved field-emitter channels along the spine, a small
compact guard with a power cell slot, a grip wrapped in black synthetic cord over a ribbed core, a pommel
with a status light. High-tech but carried and used: scuffs on the guard, worn grip wrap.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Tip` at the blade tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on the grip at the origin, the blade along +X, edge towards -Z).
