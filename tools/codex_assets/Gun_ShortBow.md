# Gun_ShortBow (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_ShortBow, a tribal short bow, ~1.0 m tip to tip, ~0.8 kg. A simple self bow of one curved stave of
pale yew-like wood, a leather-wrapped grip with a little fur, nocks carved at the tips, a twisted sinew string
(straight, braced, not drawn), a few feathers tied below the grip. Hand-made: uneven carving marks, darker
handled grip, a nick or two.
Separate parts: `Body`, `String`. Empties `StringTop` and `StringBottom` at the nocks, `Nock` at the string's
centre (where the arrow sits).
Axis: hand-weapon convention, held upright: palm on the grip at the origin, the bow's back facing +X (the
target), the string towards -X, limbs along Z.
