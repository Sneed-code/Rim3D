# Gun_ShortBow (budget 5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_ShortBow, a simple self bow cut from one stave, ~1.15 m tip to tip, ~0.8 kg. The first bow a
colonist makes at a crafting spot: a single piece of tropical hardwood (a slight D-section, bark left on the back in
places), gentle even curve with no recurve, carved nocks at the tips, the grip bound with plaited plant fibre cord,
a twisted gut or fibre bowstring at rest (straight), and one arrow (~0.7 m, cane shaft, knapped stone or bone point
bound with sinew, two split-leaf fletchings) as its own object. Knife marks, darkened grip, a small crack lashed
with cord. Clearly cruder and shorter than the laminated recurve bow.
Separate parts: `Body` (stave), `String` (pivot at its middle, so it can be pulled back), `Arrow` (pivot at its
nock). Empties `StringTop` and `StringBottom` at the string ends.
Axis: hand-weapon convention with the bow held upright: the grip at the origin, the limbs along Z, the arrow
pointing +X.
