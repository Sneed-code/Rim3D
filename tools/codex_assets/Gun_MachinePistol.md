# Gun_MachinePistol (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_MachinePistol, a compact fully automatic machine pistol, ~0.30 m long (stock folded), ~1.6 kg.
A boxy stamped-steel receiver, a short barrel with a threaded muzzle and a small compensator, a long box
magazine in the pistol grip, a folding wire stock folded along the right side, a fold-down front grip
lever, simple peep sights, a cocking knob on top. Grey-black paint worn to bare steel on edges, scratched
polymer grip, a twist of tape on the magazine base.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Magazine` (pivot at the magazine well, drops along -Z), `Bolt` (the cocking knob
and bolt, slides along X), `Trigger` (pivot at its pin). Empty `Muzzle` at the barrel tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
