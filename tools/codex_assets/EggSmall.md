# EggSmall (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: eggs from farm birds and egg-laying animals (chicken, duck, turkey and the like), a stack item lying on the ground. A small clutch of four eggs (each ~0.055 m long) resting in a loose handful of dry straw and grass, ~0.15 x 0.13 x 0.07 m. A mix of warm light-brown and cream-white shells with faint speckles, one with a tiny feather-free smudge of dirt; matte shells with a faint sheen. Straw strands really modelled around and under the eggs.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
