# Gun_ChargeRifle (budget 16k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_ChargeRifle, a spacer-tech charge rifle that fires pulsed energy-charged rounds, ~0.85 m
long, ~3.5 kg. A sleek body of white/graphite composite with machined alloy parts, a slab-sided barrel
shroud with cooling slots showing a faint blue emissive glow inside, a transparent power cell window on
the side with a blue charge strip, a compact holographic sight, a pistol grip, a cell magazine behind the
grip. Clearly more advanced than the colony guns, but used: scuffs on the composite, grime in the seams,
worn grip texture.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Magazine` (the power cell, pivot at its well), `Trigger` (pivot at its pin).
Empty `Muzzle`. Emissive map for the glows.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
