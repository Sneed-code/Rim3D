# EggLarge (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: a big egg from a large bird (goose, ostrich, cassowary), a stack item lying on the ground. One large egg (~0.15 m long, ~0.12 m wide) lying on its side in a small bed of dry grass and leaves, a second smaller one half behind it, ~0.20 x 0.16 x 0.12 m. Thick cream-ivory shell covered in fine pores (normal map), a faint greenish tint on the smaller one, a little soil on the underside. Matte shell with a slight sheen.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
