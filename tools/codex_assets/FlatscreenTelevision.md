# FlatscreenTelevision (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: FlatscreenTelevision, a wide flat-panel television on a stand that colonists watch, 2 x 1
cells, ~1.9 m wide, ~1.3 m tall with its stand. A thin-bezel panel (~1.8 x 1.0 m) on a low wooden or steel
media cabinet with two doors and a shelf holding a dusty receiver box; cables bundled down the back.
Light scuffs on the cabinet, dust on the bezel top, a small chip in one bezel corner. Screen faintly
emissive (soft blue-grey glow, no picture).
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Screen` at the screen centre.
Axis: building convention, the screen facing -Y, the long side along X.
