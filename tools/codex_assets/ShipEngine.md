# ShipEngine (budget 30k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: ShipEngine, the main thruster of the colonists' escape starship, built on a 3 x 4 m footprint
(3 wide along X, 4 deep along Y), ~3.2 m tall. A heavy industrial-sci-fi rocket engine lying horizontal on a
steel cradle: a big bell nozzle (~2.4 m across at the mouth) pointing towards +Y, a thick combustion chamber
and turbopump assembly behind it towards -Y, fuel and coolant pipes in bundles, gimbal actuators, armoured
access panels with hazard stencils, a bolted mounting frame to the ground. Inside the nozzle a faint blue
emissive glow ring (emissive mask). Scorched heat-tint on the nozzle (blue/purple/straw bands), soot, grimy
panels, worn yellow-black stripes, rivets. Must read as a real, heavy piece of machinery.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Nozzle` (gimbal pivot where it meets the chamber). Empty `Exhaust` at the nozzle
mouth centre.
Axis: building convention, nozzle mouth towards +Y (the back), the pump end towards -Y (the front).
