# SimpleResearchBench (budget 18k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: SimpleResearchBench, a 3 x 2 m low-tech research desk used by early colonists, ~1.5 m tall at
the back. A sturdy wooden work table with a back board, stacks of salvaged books and notebooks, hand-drawn
charts pinned up, a brass microscope, an oil lamp, glass jars of samples, a chalk slate, a magnifying glass,
a wooden stool tucked under (separate). Hand-made frontier look: oiled worn wood, nails, ink stains.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Stool` (origin at its bottom centre).
Axis: building convention, the working side towards -Y.
