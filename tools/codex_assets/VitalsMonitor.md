# VitalsMonitor (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: VitalsMonitor, a medical vitals monitor that stands beside a hospital bed, one 1 x 1 m cell,
~1.5 m tall. A wheeled IV-pole style stand (five-caster base, steel pole) carrying a monitor unit: an
off-white plastic housing with a dark glass screen showing glowing green/cyan heart-rate and oxygen traces
(emissive), a row of soft buttons and a dial, a coiled sensor cable and a finger-clip probe hanging off a
hook, a small basket. Hospital clean but used: yellowed plastic, tape on a cracked corner, scuffed casters.
Emissive map for the screen and a small status LED.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Screen` at the screen centre.
Axis: building convention, the screen facing -Y.
