# Flake (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: flake, a cheap, crudely made hard drug, a stack item. A small crumpled clear plastic baggie folded over and tied with a twist, holding off-white to faintly yellow crystalline flakes and shards, two or three loose shards beside it, ~0.10 x 0.07 x 0.03 m. The plastic is slightly cloudy and creased with a grimy fingerprint smudge; the shards are glassy and irregular. Plastic a little translucent (use alpha or a cloudy look in BaseColor, no glass shader needed).
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
