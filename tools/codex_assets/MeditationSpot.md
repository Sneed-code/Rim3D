# MeditationSpot (budget 2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: MeditationSpot, a marked place to sit and meditate, 1 x 1 cell, flat on the ground. A round woven
pandanus-leaf mat (~0.75 m across, spiral weave) with a small flat cushion on it, a low ring of eight smooth river
stones round the mat's edge, a small clay dish with a burnt-down incense stick and ash at one side (towards +Y).
Sun-faded weave, dirt on the stones. Very low: nothing above 0.12 m except the incense stub.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, centred on the origin, bottom at z = 0, the front facing -Y.
