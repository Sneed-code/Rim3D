# Column (budget 4k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Column, a decorative stone column that holds up a roof, one 1 x 1 m cell, 3.0 m tall (wall height).
A classical column: square base block, fluted round shaft ~0.45 m across with slight entasis, a simple
capital with a square abacus at the top. Warm grey stone (not tinted by the game), chisel marks on the base,
dust in the flutes.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, bottom at z = 0.
