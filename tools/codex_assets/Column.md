# Column (budget 4k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Column, a decorative structural column, 1 x 1 cell, 3.0 m tall (one storey, wall height). A square base
block (~0.6 m), a slightly tapered round shaft (~0.35 m across) with shallow vertical fluting, and a simple square
capital at the top touching the ceiling. Hand-dressed with chisel marks, edges a little chipped, dirt and splash
staining near the bottom, faint moss streaks (tropical damp). It takes a stuff tint (wood, stone or metal), so keep
the base colour a fairly neutral light stone with the detail in the normal and AO maps.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, centred on the origin, bottom at z = 0.
