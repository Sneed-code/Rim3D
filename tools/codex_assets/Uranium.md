# Uranium (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: uranium, a very heavy dense metal mined from ore, used for slug guns and armour. A small pile of three or four chunky, roughly cast uranium ingots/slugs with rounded edges and a cluster of raw nuggets, ~0.22 x 0.15 x 0.08 m. Dark gunmetal grey with a dull olive-green-black oxide tarnish, a few freshly chipped bright silvery-grey spots, pitted cast surface, heavy and dense looking. One ingot has a faded yellow-and-black radiation-trefoil-style stencil (generic, partly worn off). No glow.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
