# Hops (budget 2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: hops, the harvested crop used for brewing beer, a stack item. A small heap of fresh pale green hop cones (papery overlapping bracts, ~3-4 cm each, real modelled cone shapes) with a few short twining bine stems and two or three serrated leaves, ~0.22 x 0.16 x 0.08 m. Slight yellow lupulin dust between the bracts, a couple of cones turning tan at the tips. Alpha-cut leaves are fine.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
