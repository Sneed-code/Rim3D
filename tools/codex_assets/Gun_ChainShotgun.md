# Gun_ChainShotgun (budget 16k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_ChainShotgun, a magazine-fed automatic combat shotgun, ~0.95 m long, ~4.5 kg. A bulky
receiver, a 12-gauge class barrel with a ventilated heat shield and a ported muzzle brake, a large drum
magazine (~20 shells) under the receiver, a pistol grip and a forward grip on a rail, an adjustable stock
with a rubber butt pad, a carry handle with a simple sight. Matte black polymer and dark steel, scuffed,
a strip of olive tape on the forward grip, red tropical mud dried in the crevices.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Magazine` (the drum, pivot at the magazine well), `Bolt` (charging handle,
slides along X), `Trigger` (pivot at its pin). Empty `Muzzle` at the brake tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
