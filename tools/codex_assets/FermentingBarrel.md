# FermentingBarrel (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: FermentingBarrel, where wort ferments into beer, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.95 m tall, ~0.8 m wide.
A big oak cask lying on its side on a simple wooden cradle (or standing on end on a low pallet, pick the
one that fills the cell better): curved staves with visible grain and slightly uneven joins, three or four
iron hoops with rust and rivets, a wooden tap/spigot near the bottom of the front head with a drip stain,
a bung with a cloth airlock on top, chalk tally marks on the head. Damp dark stain at the base.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Tap` at the spigot mouth.
Axis: building convention, the tap facing -Y.
