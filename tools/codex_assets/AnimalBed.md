# AnimalBed (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: AnimalBed, a soft round bed that fits any tame animal, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.25 m tall, ~0.92 m
across. A round cushioned pet bed: a thick stuffed bolster ring of heavy canvas with stitched seams and a
slight sag, a separate soft cushion in the middle with a body-shaped dent, fur and a little dirt on it.
Cloth a neutral oatmeal/grey so the game can tint it by material.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention.
