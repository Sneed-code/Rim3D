# ChemfuelGenerator (budget 20k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: ChemfuelGenerator, a 2 x 2 m fuel-burning power generator, ~1.6 m tall. A diesel-genset style
machine on a skid: engine block with cylinder heads and an exhaust pipe with heat wrap and a rain cap, a
radiator with a fan grille, an alternator housing, a fuel tank with a filler cap and a gauge, a small control
panel with an hour meter, cable output box, oil and fuel stains, soot around the exhaust, faded yellow-green
paint.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Fan` (spins about its axle; pivot on it). Empty `Exhaust` at the exhaust pipe tip.
Axis: building convention, the control panel side towards -Y.
