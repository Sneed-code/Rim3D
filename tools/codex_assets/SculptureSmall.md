# SculptureSmall (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: SculptureSmall, a small art piece colonists carve, on one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.9 m tall. A carved stone
figure on a short plinth: an abstract, flowing figure of a kneeling person holding a seed or small orb up to
the sky, polished in places and rough-chiselled in others, tool marks visible. Neutral pale stone (the game
does not tint the mesh, so a warm grey marble-like stone). A little dust in the crevices.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the figure facing -Y.
