# SculptureSmall (budget 12k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: SculptureSmall, a small art piece a colonist carved, 1 x 1 cell, ~0.9 m tall including its plinth. An
original jungle-inspired carving: a crouching jaguar-like big cat curled round a tree root, its tail wrapping the
base, a few broad leaves carved under it, on a low square plinth (~0.45 m square, ~0.2 m tall) with a chipped
edge. Real sculpted shape (fur grooves, muscles, eye sockets, leaf veins in the normal map), chisel marks, dust and
dirt in the deep grooves, a little moss on the plinth's north side, polished high points where hands touch it.
It takes a stuff tint (wood, stone or metal), so keep the base colour a fairly neutral light stone with the detail
in the normal and AO maps.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the front facing -Y.
