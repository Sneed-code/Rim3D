# BionicArm (budget 12k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: BionicArm, an advanced prosthetic arm item lying on the ground or a shelf (not worn), ~0.75 m
from the shoulder socket to the fingertips. A sleek arm of white/graphite composite shell plates over dark
synthetic muscle bundles and visible actuators at the elbow and wrist, a five-fingered hand with jointed
segments slightly curled, a shoulder interface socket with gold pins and a sealed cable port, small blue
emissive status lights on the forearm. Light scratches, a dust film. Lying on its back, slightly bent at
the elbow.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, the arm along X, the hand towards +X).
