# Barricade (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Barricade, a rough cover wall built from scrap, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~1.0 m tall. A barricade that
fills the cell along X (so neighbours join into a line), ~0.4 m thick along Y: a frame of heavy timber posts
with nailed planks, a dented corrugated steel sheet and an old car door panel bolted on the front, a couple
of sandbags and rubble at the foot, wire lashings. Weathered wood, rusty steel with chipped paint, bullet
dings and scorch marks on the front face.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the line runs along X, bottom at z = 0, the armoured face towards -Y.
