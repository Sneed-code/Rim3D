# Campfire (budget 4k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Campfire, an open fire for cooking and warmth, 1 x 1 cell, ~0.3 m tall without the spit. A ring of
fire-blackened stones around a bed of ash and half-burnt logs, with a simple wooden spit (two forked sticks and a
cross bar), a blackened pot hanging. The game adds the flames, so model the wood and embers (emissive cracks in the
coals) only.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Flame` at the fire centre, just above the logs. Emissive for the embers.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
