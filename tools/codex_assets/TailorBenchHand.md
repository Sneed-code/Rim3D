# TailorBenchHand (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: TailorBenchHand, a simple non-powered tailoring workstation, 3 x 1 cells (2.9 x 0.9 m footprint),
~0.9 m to the table top. A long rough wooden worktable, a foot-treadle sewing machine (cast iron frame, treadle,
belt and hand wheel), a cutting area with fabric laid out and chalk lines, shears, a wooden yardstick, spools of
thread in a box, a rolled hide, a small stool tucked under. Worn wood, cloth scraps, dust.
Separate parts: `Body`, `HandWheel` (pivot on its axle).
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
