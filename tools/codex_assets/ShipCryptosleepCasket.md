# ShipCryptosleepCasket (budget 20k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: ShipCryptosleepCasket, the cold-sleep pod colonists slept in on the slow ship (1 x 2 m footprint,
long side along Y, ~1.1 m tall). A heavy coffin-like pod on a plinth: a curved frosted glass canopy over a
padded body-shaped bed (empty), thick insulated hoses and cables into the plinth, a side control panel with a
small screen and status lights (emissive mask; cold blue), frost and condensation stains, scuffed off-white
and grey spacer panels, worn grab handles, a faded crew number stencil. Original design.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Canopy` (glass, pivot along its hinge on the +X long edge so it can swing open; its
own transmissive frosted material). Empty `Sleeper` at the bed's centre, 0.6 m up.
Axis: building convention, the control panel side towards -Y (a short end).
