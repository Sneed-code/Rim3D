# Melee_BreachAxe (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Melee_BreachAxe, a heavy two-handed axe for chopping through doors and walls, ~1.1 m long,
~3 kg. A wide forged steel axe head with a broad slightly curved bit and a pick/hammer back, a wooden or
fibreglass haft with an overstrike guard below the head and a rubber grip near the end, a wrist cord.
Edge freshly ground bright, head painted dark and chipped, haft scuffed and dirty.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Head` at the axe head centre.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (the lower hand's palm on the grip at the haft end at the origin, the haft
along +X, the head at the +X end, the cutting edge towards -Z).
