# AnimalSleepingBox (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: AnimalSleepingBox, a wooden box with bedding for a small tame animal (cat, dog, chicken), one
1 x 1 m cell, ~0.35 m tall. A low open crate of rough planks ~0.85 x 0.85 m with a dip cut in the front
board to step over, nails, worn rounded corners, chew marks; inside a lumpy folded blanket and straw, a
few loose hairs and feathers on the blanket.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the low entrance facing -Y.
