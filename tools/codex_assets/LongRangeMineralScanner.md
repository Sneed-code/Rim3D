# LongRangeMineralScanner (budget 22k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: LongRangeMineralScanner, a 3 x 3 m powered scanner that finds ore far away, ~3 m tall. A tripod
or quad-leg steel mast carrying a rotating radar/seismic dish array, a ground sensor spike, an equipment
cabinet with a screen showing a map sweep (emissive), heavy cables, guy wires to ground anchors, warning
stripes, sun-faded white and orange paint, rust at the feet.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Dish` (spins about Z; pivot on the mast axis).
Axis: building convention, the cabinet towards -Y.
