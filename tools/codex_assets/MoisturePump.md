# MoisturePump (budget 14k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: MoisturePump, a 2 x 2 m powered pump that dries out marsh ground around it, ~1.4 m tall. A
squat rugged pump unit on a steel frame: an electric motor and pump head, a vertical well casing going into the
ground, a discharge pipe and hose, a pressure gauge, a valve wheel, a small control box, mud and water stains,
rust, tropical moss at the base.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Water` at the discharge outlet.
Axis: building convention, the control box towards -Y.
