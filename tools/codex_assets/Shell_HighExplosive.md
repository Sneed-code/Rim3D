# Shell_HighExplosive (budget 4k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Shell_HighExplosive, a mortar bomb colonists load into the mortar, ~0.42 m long, ~81 mm across.
A teardrop body painted olive drab with a yellow band and small generic stencils, a screw-in nose fuze in
brass/steel, a tail boom with a finned stabiliser and beige propellant charge bags around the boom, a
primer at the base. Dents, chipped paint, a little dirt. Also add `ShellStack`: three shells lying in a
small open wooden crate (joined, ~10k tris allowed for the stack).
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, shell lying along X, nose towards +X).
