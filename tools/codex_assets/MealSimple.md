# MealSimple (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: a simple meal, cooked by a colonist at a stove from one kind of food, a stack item lying on the ground or a table. A hand-carved wooden bowl (~0.20 m across, ~0.09 m tall with food, worn rim, tool marks, darkened by use) filled with a plain frontier stew: chunky boiled potato and root pieces, a few beans and corn kernels, a bit of meat, in a thick brownish broth with a glossy surface, a carved wooden spoon resting in it with its handle on the rim. Food really modelled as separate chunks breaking the broth surface; broth wet and glossy (low roughness), food matte to moist, wood matte. Appetising but rustic, tropical frontier cooking.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
