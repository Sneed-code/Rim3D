# Gun_HeavySMG (budget 14k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_HeavySMG, a heavy submachine gun firing big slow rounds, ~0.70 m long, ~3.8 kg. A thick
round receiver tube with a perforated barrel jacket, a big straight box magazine in front of the trigger,
a vertical front pistol grip, a pistol grip with a thumb safety, a fixed skeleton or wooden stock, a side
cocking handle, a flip rear sight. Old military surplus: parkerised steel, worn dark wood or brown
bakelite, oil stains, a scratched serial-plate area with no readable text.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Magazine` (pivot at the magazine well), `Bolt` (cocking handle, slides along X),
`Trigger` (pivot at its pin). Empty `Muzzle` at the barrel tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
