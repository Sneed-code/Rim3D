# Gun_Minigun (budget 24k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_Minigun, a man-portable six-barrel rotary machine gun carried at the hip, ~1.0 m long,
~22 kg. A cluster of six barrels held by two clamp rings, a motor housing behind the barrels, a drive
housing with a big carry handle on top, a trigger grip under the housing and a second handle beside it,
a flexible feed chute coming in from the left side (cut off at ~25 cm, ending in a clean cap), a battery
pack. Gunmetal and black, oily, heat-discoloured barrel tips, scratched.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Barrels` (the six barrels and clamps, pivot on their common spin axis along X),
`Trigger` (pivot at its pin). Empty `Muzzle` at the centre of the barrel tips.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on the rear trigger grip at the origin).
