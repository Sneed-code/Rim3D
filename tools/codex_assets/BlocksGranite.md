# BlocksGranite (budget 2.5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: granite blocks, a building resource, a stack item lying on the ground (bricks cut from rock chunks at a stonecutter's table). A neat small stack of six to eight rough-cut rectangular granite blocks (each ~0.20 x 0.10 x 0.08 m, slightly irregular, chisel-dressed faces with tool marks, chipped corners), two layers crosswise plus one block leaning, ~0.42 x 0.30 x 0.20 m overall. Speckled pinkish-grey granite with white quartz and black mica flecks, stone dust on the top faces. Each block really modelled with bevels, small gaps between them; rough, not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
