# ElectricStove (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: ElectricStove, a powered cooking range, 3 x 1 cells (2.9 x 0.9 m footprint), ~0.9 m to the cook top.
A rugged steel range with four coil hotplates (two glowing faint orange), an oven door with a window, knobs and a
pilot lamp, a steel side counter with a sink, pots and a pan, a cutting board with food, a utensil rail, a power
cable at the back. Brushed and painted steel, grease, dents, sun-faded paint.
Separate parts: `Body`. Emissive for the glowing hotplates and lamp.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
