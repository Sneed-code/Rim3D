# MultiAnalyzer (budget 20k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: MultiAnalyzer, a 2 x 2 m research machine placed beside a hi-tech research bench, ~1.6 m tall.
A boxy lab instrument cabinet: a sealed sample chamber with a round port and viewing window, a robotic sample
carousel inside, stacked analysis modules, a rack of vials, a side display and keypad, cooling vents and a
fan, cable bundles, clean white/light-grey panels with a teal accent and worn edges, coloured status lamps
(emissive mask).
Separate parts: `Body`, `Carousel` (spins about Z; pivot on its axis).
Axis: building convention, the display side towards -Y.
