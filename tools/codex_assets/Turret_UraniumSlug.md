# Turret_UraniumSlug (budget 32k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: Turret_UraniumSlug, a 2 x 2 m heavy automatic turret firing depleted-uranium slugs, ~2.4 m tall.
A long-barrelled high-velocity cannon (a rail-or-coil-like barrel with ribbed accelerator rings, faint blue
emissive coils) in an angular armoured gun house on a heavy armoured base with access hatch, a big sensor
block, an ammunition magazine, heat vents, dark gunmetal and graphite armour with hazard markings, wear and
soot. Distinct from the autocannon: sleeker, more advanced, longer barrel.
Separate parts: `Base`, `Head` (yaw pivot on the vertical axis), `Gun` (pitch pivot), `Barrel` (recoil, pivot
at its rear). Empty `Muzzle` at the barrel tip.
Axis: building convention, barrel towards -Y at rest.
