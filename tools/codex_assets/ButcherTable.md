# ButcherTable (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: ButcherTable, a workstation where colonists butcher animal carcasses, 2 x 1 cells (1.9 x 0.9 m
footprint), ~0.9 m tall. A heavy thick-topped hardwood table on squat legs with a steel-sheeted end, a deep
blood-dark end-grain chopping block set into it, a cleaver stuck in the block, knives and a bone saw on a rail, a
meat hook rack at the back with two hanging hooks, a drain gutter and a bucket underneath. Scrubbed but stained
wood, dried blood in the grain (dark, not cartoon red), dents and knife scars, worn steel.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `WorkSpot` at the cutting surface centre.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
