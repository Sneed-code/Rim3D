# TorchLamp (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: TorchLamp, a wooden torch stand that gives light, 1 x 1 cell, ~1.4 m tall. A rough wooden post set in a
small pile of stones, with an iron cup/cage at the top holding an oil-soaked rag wrap (the game adds the flame),
leather binding, charring on the top of the post.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Flame` where the flame sits (about 1.35 m up). The flame itself is NOT modelled.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
