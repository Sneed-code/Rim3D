# SmokeleafLeaves (budget 1.2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: smokeleaf leaves, the dried leaves of the smokeleaf plant, rolled into joints later. A bundle of broad serrated dried leaves (each ~15-20 cm, five-to-seven-fingered palmate shape, olive to yellow-brown, crinkled and papery) laid in a small stack and tied with a strip of faded red cloth, ~0.22 x 0.16 x 0.06 m. A few loose crumbled leaf bits around the base. Matte, dry, slightly translucent-looking colour (no subsurface needed). Real thin geometry, not flat cards.

Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
