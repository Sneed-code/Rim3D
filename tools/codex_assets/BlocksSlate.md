# BlocksSlate (budget 2.5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: slate blocks, a building resource, a stack item lying on the ground. A neat small stack of six to eight cut slate blocks (each ~0.20 x 0.10 x 0.08 m, made of visible thin laminated layers along their length, crisp edges with a few flaked corners), two layers crosswise plus one leaning, ~0.42 x 0.30 x 0.20 m overall. Dark blue-grey to charcoal with rusty iron stains on some, pale scratches. Each block really modelled with bevels, small gaps between; split faces a little smoother (G ~0.6), not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
