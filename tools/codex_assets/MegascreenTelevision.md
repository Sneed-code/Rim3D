# MegascreenTelevision (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: MegascreenTelevision, a huge wall-size screen colonists watch together, 3 x 1 cells, ~2.9 m wide,
~1.9 m tall with its stand. A very thin-bezel panel (~2.8 x 1.6 m) on a low wide media console with speakers
built in at both ends and cable management at the back. The screen shows a dim generic landscape (emissive
mask, not too bright). Satin black and dark wood, dust on the top edge, fingerprints on the console.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Screen` at the screen centre.
Axis: building convention, the screen facing -Y.
