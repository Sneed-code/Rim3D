# DoubleBed (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: DoubleBed, a bed for two, 2 x 2 cells, ~1.9 m wide, ~2.0 m long. A solid wooden frame with a
plain headboard, a thick mattress, a fitted sheet, a rumpled woven blanket folded back, two pillows, a
small rag rug half under one side. Homely frontier furniture: hand-planed wood with tool marks, faded
cloth, a stain on the blanket. The frame's wood should take a stuff tint, so keep it fairly neutral.
Separate parts: `Frame` (wood), `Bedding` (mattress, sheets, blanket, pillows). Empties `Sleeper1` and
`Sleeper2` at the two sleeping spots on the mattress top.
Axis: building convention, the head end towards +Y, the foot towards -Y.
