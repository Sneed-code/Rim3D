# TransportPod (budget 22k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: TransportPod, a one-cell (1 x 1 m footprint, ~1.9 m tall) reusable cargo drop pod that is
loaded, launched from a pod launcher and lands elsewhere. A rugged capsule: a blunt heat-shielded base
cone (scorched and streaked), a cylindrical or faceted body ~1 m across with structural ribs, a side cargo
door with a handle and a small window, three short landing legs/shock struts, small attitude thruster
nozzles near the top, a lifting ring/hard point on top, stencilled warning stripes. Dirty white and
orange paint, heat-blackened below.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Door` (pivot on its hinge side), `Legs` (as one object, pivot at the body so they
can fold). Empty `Thrusters` at the base centre for the landing flame.
Axis: building convention, the door towards -Y.
