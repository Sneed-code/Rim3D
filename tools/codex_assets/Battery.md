# Battery (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Battery, a power storage unit, 1 x 2 cells (0.94 x 1.94 m footprint), ~0.8 m tall. A rugged steel cabinet
of stacked cell modules with heavy cable terminals, cooling louvres, a charge-level gauge of five lamp segments,
hazard stripes, conduit at one end. Industrial paint, chipped, dust, a little corrosion bloom at the terminals.
Separate parts: `Body`, `ChargeLamps` (the five lamp segments, own emissive material). Long side along Y.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
