# ComponentSpacer (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: ComponentSpacer, the rare "advanced component" (spacer tech), about 0.14 x 0.10 x 0.05 m. A
sealed module with a smooth dark ceramic/composite shell, a thin glowing blue line around its seam and a
small emissive status dot, a gold contact edge connector at one end, a window showing a tiny lattice of
crystal circuitry, a heat-spreader plate with micro fins. Should read as clearly more advanced than the
industrial component (which is chunky and greasy). Also add `ComponentSpacerStack`: four of them in a loose
pile (joined, ~6k tris allowed).
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
