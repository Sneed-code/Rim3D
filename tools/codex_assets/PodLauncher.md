# PodLauncher (budget 18k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: PodLauncher, a 1 x 2 m fuelled launch rig beside which a transport pod sits; it fires the pod into
orbit. ~2.2 m tall. A steel launch gantry/clamp frame with a fuel line arm that reaches over to the pod's cell,
a chemfuel tank with a filler, pipework and valves, a launch control box with a big guarded switch, blast-
scorched base plate, warning stripes. Leave the neighbouring cell (towards +X) empty for the pod.
Separate parts: `Body`, `FuelArm` (pivot where it meets the gantry).
Axis: building convention, the long side along Y, control box towards -Y.
