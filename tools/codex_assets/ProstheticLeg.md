# ProstheticLeg (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: prosthetic leg, a simple industrial mechanical leg prosthesis, an item lying on the ground (not worn), ~0.95 m from the thigh socket to the toe. A moulded plastic thigh socket with padding and straps, a steel knee hinge with a coil-spring damper, a steel pylon tube, a rubber-soled flexible composite foot. Olive/beige paint chipped to bare metal, grease, scuffed sole. Lying on its side, knee straight.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, the leg along X, the foot towards +X).
