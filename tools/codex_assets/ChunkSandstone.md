# ChunkSandstone (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: a sandstone rock chunk, a heavy loose boulder left by mining or a collapse, lying on the ground. ~0.62 x 0.50 x 0.30 m, flat-ish bottom. Soft, wind- and rain-rounded sandstone: slab-like with crumbly broken edges, fine cross-bedding lines sweeping across the faces, a few honeycomb weathering hollows, sand grains loosened at the edges. Warm ochre to rust-red bands with paler buff layers, darker iron streaks. Grainy sandpaper normal detail; rough (G ~0.9), not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
