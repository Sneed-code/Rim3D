# FoamTurret (budget 12k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: FoamTurret, a 1 x 1 m automatic fire-fighting turret that sprays foam on fires, ~1.3 m tall. A
short pedestal with a red pressurised foam tank, a swivel head with a wide fan nozzle (not a gun), a small
heat sensor eye, a pressure gauge, a hose loop, safety-red paint with white stencils and wear.
Separate parts: `Base`, `Head` (yaw pivot on the vertical axis), `Nozzle` (pitch pivot). Empty `Spray` at the
nozzle tip.
Axis: building convention, nozzle towards -Y at rest.
