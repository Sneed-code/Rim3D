# Mortar (budget 18k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Turret_Mortar, a crewed indirect-fire mortar emplacement on a 2 x 2 m footprint. A ~81 mm
smoothbore mortar tube (~1.3 m long) on a round steel base plate (~0.6 m) with ground spades, a bipod
with elevation screw and traverse handwheel, a small dial sight, all sitting inside a shallow ring of
filled sandbags (half height, ~0.5 m, a gap at the back for the crew) on the 2 x 2 footprint. Next to it a
small open wooden crate with three mortar shells (separate object). Army-green paint chipped to steel,
sandbags in faded burlap with sagging realistic shapes.
Separate parts: `Base` (sandbags, base plate, crate), `Tube` (pivot where the tube meets the base plate,
so it can be aimed in pitch and yaw), `Bipod` (pivot at the collar on the tube), `Shell` (one loose shell,
~0.5 m, origin at its centre). Empty `Muzzle` at the tube mouth.
Axis: building convention, the tube leaning ~60 deg up towards -Y.
