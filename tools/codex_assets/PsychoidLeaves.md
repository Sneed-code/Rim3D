# PsychoidLeaves (budget 1.2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: psychoid leaves, the raw harvested leaves of the psychoid plant (a tropical shrub), the source of stimulant drugs. A loose bundle of long, glossy, deep blue-green spear-shaped leaves (each ~18-22 cm, wavy edges, pale central vein) tied at the stems with a twist of dry grass twine, lying on the ground, ~0.24 x 0.14 x 0.07 m. Some leaves curled and slightly wilted, a couple with brown dry tips and small insect-nibbled holes (alpha cut-outs or real holes). Leaves are real thin geometry with slight thickness, not flat cards.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
