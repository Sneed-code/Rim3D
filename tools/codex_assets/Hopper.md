# Hopper (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Hopper, a feed hopper that sits next to the nutrient paste dispenser and holds raw food for it,
one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.9 m tall. A square steel bin wider at the top than the bottom (a funnel), on four short
legs or a frame, open top with a rolled lip, a slide gate and a short chute at the bottom on the side that
feeds the dispenser, bolted seams, rivets, dented sides, food grime and rust streaks inside the lip.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Chute` at the chute outlet.
Axis: building convention, the chute side facing +Y (the dispenser is behind it); the front is -Y.
