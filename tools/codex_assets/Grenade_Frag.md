# Grenade_Frag (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Grenade_Frag, a thrown fragmentation grenade, ~11 cm tall, ~0.4 kg. An egg-shaped segmented cast-iron
body (deep pineapple grooves) painted olive drab, a steel fuse assembly on top with a curved spoon down the side and
a pull pin with a ring through it, a small yellow band below the fuse. Paint chipped to dark iron on the ridges, a
little rust in the grooves, a scuffed spoon.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Spoon` (pivot at its hinge), `Pin` (the pull ring).
Axis: thrown-item convention (origin at centre of mass, upright along +Z).
