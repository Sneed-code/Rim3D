# AnimalSleepingSpot (budget 2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: AnimalSleepingSpot, a spot where a tame animal sleeps, 1 x 1 cell, flat on the ground. A rough loose pile
of dry grass and palm-frond litter (~0.85 m across, ~0.1 m high, hollowed in the middle where an animal lies), a few
loose stems poking out, an old torn sack under part of it. Straw and dry-leaf colours, dusty.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, centred on the origin, bottom at z = 0.
