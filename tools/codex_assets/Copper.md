# Copper (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: copper, a metal resource, a stack item. A small pile of three cast copper ingots (trapezoid bars with rounded cast edges) and a loose coil of thick copper wire, ~0.24 x 0.16 x 0.08 m. Warm salmon-orange metal with darker brown oxidation, patches of blue-green verdigris in the crevices, a few bright freshly scraped spots, slightly pitted cast top faces. Metallic in ORM.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
