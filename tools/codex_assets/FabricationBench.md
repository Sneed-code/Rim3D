# FabricationBench (budget 28k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: FabricationBench, a 5 x 2 m high-tech workbench where advanced components and bionics are made,
~1.9 m tall at the back. A long heavy workbench with an anti-static top, a precision robot arm on a rail along
the back, a small enclosed fabricator with a glass door, magnifier lamp on an arm, soldering station,
parts bins and drawers, a terminal with a screen, tool wall at the back, cable trays; worn industrial grey
with orange safety accents, emissive screen and status lights. Leave the front edge clear for a worker.
Separate parts: `Body`, `RobotArm` (pivot at its base on the rail).
Axis: building convention, the working side towards -Y.
