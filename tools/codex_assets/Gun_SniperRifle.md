# Gun_SniperRifle (budget 16k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_SniperRifle, a long-range bolt-action precision rifle, ~1.20 m long, ~6.5 kg. A long heavy
fluted barrel with a muzzle brake, a chassis or laminated-wood stock with an adjustable cheek riser and
butt spacers, a detachable box magazine, a long scope (~45 cm, 50 mm objective with a sunshade and turret
caps) on a rail, a folded bipod, a large bolt knob. Desert-tan/olive paint over the stock with wear
patches, blackened steel, a scrap of hessian wrapped round the scope.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Bolt` (pivot on the bolt axis, lifts and slides along X), `Magazine` (pivot at
the well), `Scope` (fixed, separate so it can be swapped), `Trigger` (pivot at its pin). Empty `Muzzle`
and empty `ScopeEye` at the centre of the scope's eyepiece.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
