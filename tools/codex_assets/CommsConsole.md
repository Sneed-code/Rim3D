# CommsConsole (budget 22k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: CommsConsole, a 3 x 2 m long-range radio and data console: a waist-high angled desk console
with two or three CRT-style or rugged flat screens, chunky buttons, a handset on a coiled cord, dials
and VU meters, a microphone on a gooseneck, cable bundles out of the back to a tall rack cabinet on one
end with a whip antenna mount, ventilation grilles, wear on the arm rests and keys. Off-white
and grey steel with dark green panels. Screens and indicator lamps on an emissive mask (`Emissive`
texture; screens show a dim green radar/waveform pattern).
Separate parts: `Body`, `Handset` (origin at its middle, resting in its cradle), `Screens` (just the screen
faces, so we can swap their material).
Axis: building convention, the operator side towards -Y; leave clear floor in front for a standing pawn.
