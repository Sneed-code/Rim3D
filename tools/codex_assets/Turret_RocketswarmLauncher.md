# Turret_RocketswarmLauncher (budget 28k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Turret_RocketswarmLauncher, a 2 x 2 m crewed artillery launcher that fires a swarm of small
rockets, ~2.2 m tall. A heavy turntable base with an operator seat and controls at the back, a box launcher pod
with a grid of 16 tube mouths (some with rocket noses visible) on an elevating cradle, hydraulic rams,
blast-scorched pod face, cable runs, armour plates, olive drab paint chipped and rusted, warning stripes.
Separate parts: `Base` (with seat), `Pod` (pitch pivot at its trunnions), and the turntable `Head` (yaw pivot
on the vertical axis through the origin; Pod is mounted on it). Empty `PodFace` at the centre of the tube grid.
Axis: building convention, the pod facing -Y at rest, raised ~30 degrees.
