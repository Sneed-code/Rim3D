# WatermillGenerator (budget 25k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: WatermillGenerator, a 3 x 3 m generator built over a flowing river: a big wooden waterwheel
(~2.6 m across, paddles) on an iron axle turning in two stone-and-timber bearing piers, with a small
generator house (timber frame, tin roof) on one pier, gear train and a drive belt, moss and wet dark wood near
the waterline, iron straps and bolts. The bottom ~0.6 m of the wheel will sit in water.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Wheel` (spins about its axle, which runs along X; pivot on the axle).
Axis: building convention, the generator house on the -Y side.
