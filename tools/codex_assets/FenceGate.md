# FenceGate (budget 4k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: FenceGate, a gate in an animal pen fence, 1 x 1 cell, ~1.2 m tall. Two square timber posts (~0.1 m,
~1.2 m tall) at the cell's edges, x = -0.45 and +0.45, with a hinged frame gate between them (two horizontal rails,
a diagonal brace, vertical slats), iron strap hinges on the left post, a simple drop latch on the right. The
neighbouring fence links at mid-height on both posts, along X. Hand-sawn tropical timber, weathered grey with green
algae near the ground, rusted ironwork. It takes a stuff tint, so keep the wood fairly neutral.
Separate parts: `Posts`, `Gate` (pivot on the hinge line on the left post, so it can swing open).
Axis: building convention, the gate's face towards -Y.
