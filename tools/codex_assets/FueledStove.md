# FueledStove (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: FueledStove, a wood-burning cooking stove, 3 x 1 cells (2.9 x 0.9 m footprint), ~0.9 m to the cook
top. A cast-iron range with a firebox door showing glowing embers through a grille, two iron cooking plates and a
pot and a pan on top, a short stove pipe at the back, a brick base, a side counter with a chopping board,
vegetables and a knife, utensils hanging from a rail. Soot, burnt-on grease, chipped enamel.
Separate parts: `Body`. Emissive for the embers. Empty `Flame` at the firebox centre.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
