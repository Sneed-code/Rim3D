# Gun_RecurveBow (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_RecurveBow, a hand-made recurve bow, ~1.45 m tip to tip, ~1 kg. A laminated wooden bow with
recurved tips, a leather-wrapped grip with an arrow shelf, horn or bone tip nocks, a twisted fibre
bowstring at rest (straight), and one arrow (~0.75 m, wooden shaft, iron broadhead, three feather
fletchings) as its own object. Oiled wood, dark at the grip from hands, frayed leather.
Separate parts: `Body` (bow and limbs), `String` (pivot at its middle, so it can be pulled back), `Arrow`
(pivot at its nock). Empties `StringTop` and `StringBottom` at the string ends.
Axis: hand-weapon convention with the bow held upright: the grip at the origin, the limbs along Z, the
arrow pointing +X.
