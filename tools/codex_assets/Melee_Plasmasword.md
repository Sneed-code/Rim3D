# Melee_Plasmasword (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Melee_Plasmasword, an ultratech sword whose blade is wrapped in burning plasma, ~1.0 m long.
A solid metal core blade (~0.7 m) with a central slot and emitter studs down both flats, heat-blued and
discoloured near the edges, a chunky guard with a plasma reservoir and vents, a ribbed grip, a pommel cap
with a charge ring. Emissive orange-white glow along the emitter slot and edges (the game adds the plasma
flames). Scorch marks, soot in the vents.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Tip` at the blade tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on the grip at the origin, the blade along +X, edge towards -Z).
