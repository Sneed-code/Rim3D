# HiTechResearchBench (budget 28k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: HiTechResearchBench, a 5 x 2 m laboratory bench. A long steel-framed workbench with a resin
top, an overhead shelf rail with a light bar, a microscope, a centrifuge, a sealed glovebox with a
glass front on one end, a terminal with a curved screen, racks of sample vials, a small 3D-printer-like
fabricator, cable trays and hoses underneath, a high stool tucked under (separate). Clean but used
spacer-tech look: white and light grey panels, brushed aluminium, coloured status lights (emissive mask).
Separate parts: `Body`, `Stool` (origin at the bottom centre), `Screen` (screen face only).
Axis: building convention, the working side towards -Y.
