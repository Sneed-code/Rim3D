# Penoxycyline (budget 1k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Penoxycyline, a preventive anti-malaria / anti-plague drug colonists take every few days in the tropics. A small rugged blister-card pack of pills lying flat with a short squat pill bottle beside it, ~0.14 x 0.10 x 0.07 m. The bottle: chunky translucent amber polymer with a white child-proof ridged cap, a pale green paper label band with a simple green cross and generic stencil bars (no words). The blister card: foil-backed white card with two rows of oval pale-blue tablets in clear domed pockets, two pockets already pushed out and torn. Slight humidity crinkle on the card, a thumb smudge on the bottle.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
