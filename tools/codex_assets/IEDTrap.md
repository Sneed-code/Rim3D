# IEDTrap (budget 6k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: TrapIEDExplosive, a hidden explosive trap in one 1 x 1 m cell. Two old rusty artillery shells
(~0.15 m x 0.55 m) lying side by side, half buried in a shallow scraped patch of earth with some leaf
litter, wired together with coloured wires (red, black, yellow electrical tape) to a crude pressure plate
(two boards with a tin can switch) and a small battery pack wrapped in tape. Grimy, improvised, dangerous.
Separate parts: `Body` (all), plus an empty `BlastCenter` at the shells' middle.
Axis: building convention. Keep it low (under 0.25 m) so it reads as hidden.
