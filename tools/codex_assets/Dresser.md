# Dresser (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Dresser, a chest of drawers that makes a bedroom nicer, 2 x 1 cells (1.94 x 0.94 m footprint), ~1.0 m
tall. A solid wooden dresser with six drawers in two columns, metal pulls, a top with a folded cloth, a small
mirror and a few personal things. Hand-made, oiled wood, worn edges, one slightly open drawer. Keep the wood
fairly neutral (stuff tint).
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
