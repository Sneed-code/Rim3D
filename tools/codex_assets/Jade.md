# Jade (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Jade, a pile of three or four rough-cut raw jade chunks lying on the ground, ~0.25 x 0.20 x 0.12 m. Waxy, translucent-looking green stone (deep green with paler milky veins) with flat saw-cut faces on one side and natural rough rind (brown-grey crust) on the others. Use low roughness on cut faces, high on the crust; a subtle subsurface feel through colour variation (no transmission needed).
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
