# ChunkMarble (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: a marble rock chunk, a heavy loose boulder left by mining or a collapse, lying on the ground. ~0.58 x 0.50 x 0.32 m, flat-ish bottom. Broken white marble: angular with large smooth fracture faces and sugary crystalline broken surfaces, chipped edges, one face with old saw or chisel marks from a quarry. White to very pale grey with soft grey and faint gold veins running through the whole stone (veins continue across faces), grime and green-black mildew in the low crevices, red soil dust at the base. Broken faces slightly glittery (lower roughness speckle); not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
