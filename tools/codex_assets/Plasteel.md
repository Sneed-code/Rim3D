# Plasteel (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: plasteel, a light, extremely strong advanced alloy (spacer material). A small stack of four precision-cut plasteel plates and two hexagonal bar sections stacked crosswise, ~0.24 x 0.16 x 0.08 m. Pale blue-grey satin metal with a faint cool sheen and subtle brushed/anisotropic-looking grain (via normal/roughness), crisp machined chamfers, a laser-etched generic batch mark (dots and bars, no text) on the top plate, a few light scuffs and fingerprint smears, edges slightly brighter. Looks high-tech and clean compared to steel.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
