# ShipComputerCore (budget 14k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: ShipComputerCore, the computer core of the escape starship the colonists build, 2 x 2 cells,
~1.9 x 1.9 m, ~2.2 m tall. A heavy armoured cabinet with rounded corners, a central crystal-lattice core
seen through a thick window with a blue emissive glow, cooling fins and radiator panels on the sides,
cable trunks into the floor, a small maintenance hatch and status panels. Spacer-made but assembled by
colonists: clean composite panels mixed with welded steel brackets and taped cable.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Core` at the core centre. Emissive map.
Axis: building convention, the window facing -Y.
