# HydroponicsBasin (budget 12k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: HydroponicsBasin, a powered indoor growing tray on one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.9 m tall. A waist-high
rectangular steel and plastic basin on legs, filled with dark nutrient gel/clay pebbles and a grid of planting
holes, a thin overhead grow-lamp bar on two posts (~0.6 m above the tray, pink-white LED strip on an emissive
mask), a small pump and nutrient tank under the tray with tubes, a drip line along the edge, mineral crust and
water stains, green algae at the corners. Leave the planting holes empty (our plants grow in them).
Separate parts: `Body`, `Lamp` (the light bar). Empty `PlantArea` at the centre of the gel surface.
Axis: building convention, the pump side towards -Y.
