# BiofuelRefinery (budget 25k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: BiofuelRefinery, a 3 x 2 m machine that turns plant matter into chemfuel. ~1.9 m tall: a
loading hopper with a lid on one end, a horizontal fermentation tank with sight glass showing amber
liquid (separate material), a small distillation column with coils, pumps and valves, a control panel,
an outlet spout over a filled jerry can (separate object), stained by spilled fuel and plant mush.
Industrial green-grey paint, brass valves, rusty bolts.
Separate parts: `Body`, `HopperLid` (pivot at its hinge), `JerryCan` (origin at its bottom centre).
Axis: building convention, the control panel towards -Y.
