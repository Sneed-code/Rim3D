# Cloth (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: cloth, plain woven cotton fabric used for clothes and beds. A stack of three folded bolts of undyed off-white / natural cream cotton cloth, slightly uneven folds with soft rounded edges and sagging corners, the top bolt partly unrolled with a loose draped end, ~0.32 x 0.22 x 0.12 m. Visible plain-weave texture in the normal map, a few loose threads, a faint dust smudge and a small tea-coloured stain. Soft, matte, no shine.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
