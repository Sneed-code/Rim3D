# Crematorium (budget 18k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: Crematorium, a 3 x 2 m electric cremation furnace, ~1.9 m tall. A plain, sombre industrial
furnace: brushed steel and enamel cabinet, a heavy insulated loading door with a wheel handle on a slide rail
in front of a roller tray, a small control panel, a tall exhaust stack at the back, heat-stained steel around
the door, a faint orange glow at the door seal (emissive). Dignified, not gory.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Door` (slides up along Z; pivot at its bottom centre).
Axis: building convention, the door side towards -Y.
