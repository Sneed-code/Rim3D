# ElectricSmelter (budget 20k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: ElectricSmelter, a 3 x 1 m electric arc furnace that melts slag into metal, ~1.8 m tall. A heavy
refractory-lined steel furnace with a small glowing door window (orange-hot emissive mask), a crucible tilt
lever, heavy power cables into graphite electrode housings on top, a heat shield, a control box with dials and
a big red stop button, an exhaust hood and duct, a slag bin at one end, heat discolouration (blue/straw tints)
and soot on the steel.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Door` (pivot at its top hinge).
Axis: building convention, the door side towards -Y.
