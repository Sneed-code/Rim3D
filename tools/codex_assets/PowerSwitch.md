# PowerSwitch (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: PowerSwitch, a switch on a power line that turns a circuit on or off, one 1 x 1 m cell (it sits
on a conduit). A rugged waist-high steel post (~1.1 m) with a weatherproof switch box on top: a big
knife-switch style lever with a red handle, conduit glands at the bottom, a green and a red indicator
lamp, a hazard stripe. Painted grey, chipped, a little rust round the bolts.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Lever` (pivot at its hinge). Emissive map for the lamps.
Axis: building convention, the lever facing -Y.
