# Heater (budget 5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Heater, an electric space heater that warms a room, 1 x 1 cell, ~1.1 m tall. A sturdy floor-standing
steel cabinet with a front grille over glowing orange heating elements, a top vent, a dial and a pilot lamp, a
power cable, carry handles. Painted steel, heat-discoloured above the grille, dust.
Separate parts: `Body`. Emissive for the elements and lamp.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
