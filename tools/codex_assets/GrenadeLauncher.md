# GrenadeLauncher (budget 18k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_IncendiaryLauncher, Gun_SmokeLauncher and Gun_EMPLauncher share this one model (the round's
colour band is a separate part so we can tint it). A two-handed, single-shot, break-open 40 mm-class
grenade launcher, about 0.75-0.85 m long, ~3.4 kg: thick short rifled barrel (~40 mm bore, visible rifling
at the muzzle), a hinged breech that tips down to load, pistol grip, a folding or fixed stock, a front
vertical grip or barrel shroud with grip texture, a flip-up ladder sight. Rugged colony-made / military
surplus look: parkerised steel barrel, scratched polymer or oiled hardwood furniture (pick one), sling
loops, a strip of cloth tape repair on the stock.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Barrel` (pivot on the breech hinge, so it can tip open), `Trigger` (pivot at its
pin), `Sight` (pivot at its hinge), and a loose round `Round40mm` (~40 x 100 mm: brass/aluminium case,
olive projectile, a coloured band object `Round40mm_Band`) sitting in the open chamber position.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
