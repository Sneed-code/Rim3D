# ChessTable (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: ChessTable, a recreation table for two to play a board game, 1 x 2 cells (0.94 x 1.94 m footprint),
~0.75 m tall. A sturdy wooden table with an inlaid checkered board in the middle of the top, carved pieces mid-game
(simple original carved shapes, a few captured to the side), two small drawers for pieces. Hand-made, oiled
wood, worn varnish, cup rings. Keep the wood fairly neutral (stuff tint).
Separate parts: `Body`. Empties `Player1` and `Player2` at the two long-side seats. Long side along Y.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
