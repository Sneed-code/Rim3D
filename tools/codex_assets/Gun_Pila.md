# Gun_Pila (budget 5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_Pila, a heavy throwing javelin, ~1.9 m long, ~4 kg, thrown one-handed. A long thin iron shank
(~60 cm, square section, hand-forged with hammer facets) ending in a small pyramidal armour-piercing point, socketed
and riveted into an ash-wood shaft (~1.3 m) through a squared wooden block with an iron collar; a leather-wrapped
grip at the balance point with a lead weight bulge under it; a blunt iron butt cap. Forge scale, oily black iron,
rust bloom near the rivets, worn pale wood at the grip.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Tip` at the point.
Axis: hand-weapon convention: the throwing hand's palm on the grip at the origin, the point towards +X, the shaft
along X, +Z up.
