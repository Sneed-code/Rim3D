# Gun_Pila (budget 5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_Pila, a heavy throwing javelin (Roman pilum style), ~1.6 m long, ~1.8 kg; colonists carry one and
throw it. A long thin iron shank (~0.6 m) with a small pyramidal barbed head, socketed into an ash wood shaft
with a square wooden block and iron collar at the join, a leather-wrapped grip near the balance point. Forged
iron with hammer marks and rust bloom, worn wood.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Tip` at the point.
Axis: hand-weapon convention: palm on the grip at the origin, the point along +X.
