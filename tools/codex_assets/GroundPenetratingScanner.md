# GroundPenetratingScanner (budget 20k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: GroundPenetratingScanner, a 3 x 3 m powered machine a colonist operates to find deep ore under the
colony, ~1.8 m tall. A wheeled or skid-mounted sensor sled with a big flat ground-radar antenna box low to the
ground, a control console with a screen and joystick on a stand (operator side), a generator/battery box,
cable reels, a mast with a GPS-like puck, scuffed yellow-grey industrial paint, mud.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Screen` at the console screen.
Axis: building convention, the operator console towards -Y.
