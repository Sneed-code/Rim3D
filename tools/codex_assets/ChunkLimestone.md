# ChunkLimestone (budget 3k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: a limestone rock chunk, a heavy loose boulder left by mining or a collapse, lying on the ground. ~0.60 x 0.48 x 0.30 m, flat-ish bottom. Blocky sedimentary limestone: roughly squared with visible bedding layers (horizontal bands a few cm thick), some layers stepped back where softer rock weathered out, rounded solution pits and small holes, one or two embedded fossil shell imprints. Pale warm cream-grey with darker grey water streaks, a little green-black algae stain in the pits from the humid jungle, red-brown soil caked low. Real modelled layers and pits; rough, not metallic.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
