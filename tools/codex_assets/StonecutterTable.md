# StonecutterTable (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: StonecutterTable, a workstation where colonists cut rock chunks into building blocks, 3 x 1 cells
(2.9 x 0.9 m footprint), ~0.9 m tall. A massive timber-and-steel bench with a stone-slab top, a hand-cranked
or simple motor-driven circular stone saw with a guard at one end, chisels, mallets, a square and a level on the
top, a half-cut rock chunk and a stack of three finished blocks, a water bucket for the blade. Thick stone dust in
every crevice, chipped edges, rust on the saw guard.
Separate parts: `Body`, `SawBlade` (pivot on its axle).
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
