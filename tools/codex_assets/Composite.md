# Composite (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: composite, a fibre-reinforced plastic sheet material, as tough as steel at half the weight, a stack item. A short neat stack of four thin rigid panels, ~0.40 x 0.30 x 0.06 m, with a woven carbon/glass-fibre twill weave visible under a semi-gloss resin on the faces, dark charcoal with a slight blue-grey sheen, cut edges showing pale layered laminate, one panel slightly offset, a strip of tough banding tape round the stack. Scuffs and a dusty fingerprint.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
