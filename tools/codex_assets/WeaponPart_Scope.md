# WeaponPart_Scope (budget 5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: scope, a loose rifle scope weapon part lying on the ground (an item, not mounted). A rugged mid-size optical scope ~0.30 m long: matte black anodised tube with an olive rubber armour sleeve, turrets with knurled caps, a flip-up lens cover open, glassy blue-tinted lens coating, a picatinny-style quick-release mount underneath. Scuffed edges worn to bare aluminium, dust.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, the tube along X).
