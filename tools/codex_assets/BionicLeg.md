# BionicLeg (budget 12k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: BionicLeg, an advanced prosthetic leg item lying on the ground or a shelf (not worn), ~1.0 m
from the hip socket to the toes. White/graphite composite shell plates over dark synthetic muscle,
visible actuators at the knee and ankle, a jointed foot with a rubber sole, a hip interface socket with
gold pins and a sealed cable port, small blue emissive status lights on the shin. Matches the bionic
arm's design. Light scratches, a dust film. Lying on its side, slightly bent at the knee.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, the leg along X, the foot towards +X).
