# HospitalBed (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: HospitalBed, a medical bed, 1 x 2 cells (~0.95 m wide, ~2.0 m long). A steel-framed adjustable bed
with raised head section, side rails, small castor wheels, a thin mattress with clean pale sheets and a pillow, a
folded blanket at the foot, a drip stand with a bag at the head, a clipboard hooked on the foot rail. Clinical but
frontier-worn: chipped white enamel, rust spots, a patched sheet. Keep the frame fairly neutral (it takes a stuff
tint).
Separate parts: `Frame`, `Bedding`. Empty `Sleeper1` at the sleeping spot on the mattress. Head end towards +Y, foot towards -Y.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
