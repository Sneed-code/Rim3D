# RawPotatoes (budget 2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: potatoes, a raw crop food, a stack item. A small heap of six to eight raw potatoes of varied size and shape (lumpy ovals 6-10 cm, real modelled bumps and eyes), ~0.24 x 0.18 x 0.10 m. Tan-brown skin with soil dirt in the creases, a couple with reddish skin, one cut in half showing pale creamy flesh. Matte.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
