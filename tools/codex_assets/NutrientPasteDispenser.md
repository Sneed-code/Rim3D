# NutrientPasteDispenser (budget 25k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: NutrientPasteDispenser, a 3 x 4 m food machine (3 wide along X, 4 deep along Y), ~2.1 m tall:
a large rectangular industrial machine of brushed and painted steel panels, a front face (-Y) with a
serving hatch and a small drip tray where a bowl of paste comes out, a simple status panel with a few
glowing buttons (emissive mask in a 4th texture `Emissive`), a big grinder hopper funnel on the top rear
fed from the back, pipes and a coolant line running along one side, service hatches, rivets, food-grade
stainless steel around the outlet, grime and food stains around the hatch. Lifted on four short feet.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Hatch` (pivot at the top hinge of the serving hatch), and a bowl `PasteBowl`
(~0.2 m, beige-grey paste) sitting in the outlet.
Axis: building convention, the serving front towards -Y.
