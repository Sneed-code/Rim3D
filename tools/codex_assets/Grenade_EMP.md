# Grenade_EMP (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Grenade_EMP, a thrown electromagnetic pulse grenade, ~12 cm tall, ~0.4 kg. A cylindrical
canister with a ribbed dark-grey casing, copper coil windings visible through slots round its middle,
blue emissive slits, a spoon and pull ring on the top fuse, a yellow-and-black warning stripe band.
Scratched paint, a dented base.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Spoon` (pivot at its hinge), `Pin` (the pull ring). Emissive map for the slits.
Axis: thrown-item convention (origin at centre of mass, upright along +Z).
