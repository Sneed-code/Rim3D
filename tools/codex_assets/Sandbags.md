# Sandbags (budget 6k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Sandbags, waist-high cover colonists shoot from, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.75 m tall. A low wall of
stacked burlap sandbags filling the cell edge to edge along X (so neighbouring cells join into one line),
~0.55 m thick along Y, 4 layers staggered like brickwork, each bag a soft pillow shape with stitched seams,
tied ends and slight sag, a few bags torn with sand spilling, some damp and darker at the bottom, dirt and
mud splashes. Realistic burlap weave in the textures.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the line runs along X, bottom at z = 0.
