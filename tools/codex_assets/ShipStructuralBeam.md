# ShipStructuralBeam (budget 16k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: ShipStructuralBeam, a section of the escape starship's main frame laid on the ground while it is
built, 2 x 6 m footprint (6 long along Y), ~1.2 m tall. A massive steel truss: two heavy I-beam chords joined
by diagonal webbing, with bolted gusset plates, lightening holes, mounting lugs at each end and cable trays
along the top. Primer-grey and hazard-yellow paint, weld seams, chalk marks and grime, some fresh bare steel.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, long along Y, bottom at z = 0.
