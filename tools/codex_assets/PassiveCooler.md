# PassiveCooler (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: PassiveCooler, a low-tech cooler that uses evaporating water, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~0.8 m tall.
A squat box: wooden frame with a clay/terracotta water tray on top, wet woven reed or cloth panels on the
sides (dark where soaked), a wooden ladle and a clay water jar beside it on the same base, a few drips and
a damp patch on the ground. Rustic, hand-built, fits a tropical camp.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the side with the jar facing -Y.
