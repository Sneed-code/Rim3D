# DeepDrill (budget 22k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: DeepDrill, a one-cell (1 x 1 m footprint) portable drilling rig, ~2.4 m tall, worked by a
colonist standing at its controls: a small A-frame/tower derrick, a drill motor head riding on a rail, a
drill string going into a bore hole in a heavy base plate, a control box with levers on the front, a
hydraulic hose bundle, a counterweight, oily mud splashed around the base and on the plate, safety
yellow paint worn through.
Separate parts: `Base` (fixed), `DrillHead` (slides along local Z on the rail; pivot at its centre),
`DrillBit` (spins about Z; pivot on the drill axis).
Axis: building convention, the controls towards -Y.
