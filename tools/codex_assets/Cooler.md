# Cooler (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Cooler, an air cooler built INTO a wall: one 1 x 1 m wall cell, the unit fills a hole through a
~0.5 m thick wall, front face ~0.8 m wide x ~0.55 m tall. Model only the unit (no wall): a boxy steel
air-conditioner chassis ~0.8 x 0.5 x 0.55 m. Front (cold side): an off-white grille with angled louvres and
a small control panel. Back (hot side): a fan guard over a large fan and a finned condenser coil, dusty and
sun-faded. Drip stain under the front, screws, service hatch.
Separate parts: `Body`, `Fan` (spin pivot on the fan axis, at the back). Put the unit's bottom at z = 0
(the game lifts it to its height in the wall).
Axis: building convention, the cold front facing -Y, the hot back facing +Y.
