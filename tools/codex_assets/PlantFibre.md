# PlantFibre (budget 2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: plant fibre, primitive woven plant and wood fibre for tribal wear, a stack item. A bundled hank of dried coarse plant fibres (sisal/coconut-coir-like, tropical), twisted and folded in half and tied with a twisted cord, beside a short section of hand-woven flat matting, ~0.30 x 0.18 x 0.07 m. Straw-gold to light brown, loose stray strands sticking out (alpha cards ok), rough matte.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
