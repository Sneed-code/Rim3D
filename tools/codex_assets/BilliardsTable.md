# BilliardsTable (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: BilliardsTable, a recreation pool table, 2 x 4 cells (1.94 x 3.94 m footprint), ~0.85 m tall. A heavy
wooden table with thick turned legs, green felt bed, rubber cushions, six leather-netted pockets, balls in play
(plain solid and striped colours, no numbers), two cues lying across one end, chalk cube. Worn felt with a
patch, scuffed wood corners. Keep the wood fairly neutral (stuff tint).
Separate parts: `Body`. Long side along Y.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
