# EndTable (budget 4k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: EndTable, a small bedside table, 1 x 1 cell (~0.45 x 0.45 m), ~0.55 m tall. A small wooden table with one
drawer and a lower shelf, a candle holder or small lamp and a book on top. Worn edges, cup rings. Keep the wood
fairly neutral (stuff tint).
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
