# Hive (budget 14k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Hive, a "brood mound": the nest of giant insect-like creatures (our chitinids), one 1 x 1 m cell,
~1.6 m tall. An organic mound of hardened resin, chewed earth and plant fibre, lumpy and ridged like a
termite mound crossed with a wasp nest, with several dark tunnel openings, glossy amber resin drips, a few
translucent egg sacs clustered near the base, root tendrils and leaf litter at the foot, moist sheen
(roughness variation). Creepy and natural, no gore. Fits a tropical rainforest floor.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Mouth` at the largest tunnel opening.
Axis: building convention, the largest opening towards -Y.
