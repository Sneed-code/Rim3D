# WeaponPart_Compensator (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: recoil compensator, a loose muzzle device weapon part lying on the ground. A stubby machined steel muzzle brake/compensator ~0.08 m long with angled ported vents on top and sides, internal threads, wrench flats, lying beside a small crush washer. Black nitride finish with carbon soot around the ports, bright machined edges. Real modelled port cuts (not just normal map).
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
