# Vent (budget 2k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: Vent, a wall vent that lets air through between two rooms, set into a wall cell (1 m wide; the
wall itself is drawn by the game, only the vent is modelled). A square steel louvred grille ~0.5 x 0.5 m
with a frame and flange, angled louvres, and a duct sleeve ~0.3 m deep behind it with a matching grille
on the other face, so it reads from both sides. Dusty, a grease streak, light rust at the bottom edge.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the grille face towards -Y, centred on the origin in X and Y, its centre at
z = 2.2 m (high on the wall).
