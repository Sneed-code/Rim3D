# Hay (budget 1.5k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: hay, cut and dried haygrass used as animal feed. A small compact rectangular bale of dry golden-green hay, ~0.40 x 0.26 x 0.22 m, bound with two strands of faded orange baling twine pressed into the bale, ends cut fairly square with loose stalks sticking out and drooping, a few stray strands lying on the ground around it. Mixed straw colours (pale gold, faded green, some grey-brown weathered stalks); real geometric stray stalks around the silhouette so it doesn't read as a box, and a strong straw normal map.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
