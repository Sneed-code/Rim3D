# SleeperShard (budget 5k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Sleeper shard, our name for an archotech-grade AI persona core: a priceless sealed machine mind, found or traded, never made. A heavy palm-sized artefact ~0.16 x 0.16 x 0.18 m: a faceted crystalline core (deep violet-blue, glassy, with fine gold circuit filaments suspended inside) held in an ornate cage of dark graphite and tarnished gold alloy with interlocking geometric plates, four short legs so it stands upright, tiny glyph-like engraved patterns (generic, not text). A slow inner glow in the crystal (emissive map, cool blue-violet). Ancient: micro scratches, dust in the engravings.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre).
