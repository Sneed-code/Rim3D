# Gun_LMG (budget 20k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: Gun_LMG, a belt-fed light machine gun, ~1.10 m long, ~8 kg. A long receiver with a hinged feed
tray cover, a heavy barrel with a carry handle and a cone flash hider, a folding bipod under the gas block
(legs folded forward along the barrel), a box ammunition pouch hanging under the receiver with a short
linked-cartridge belt feeding in, a pistol grip, a solid stock with a sling loop. Dark green paint over
steel, worn bright on the carry handle and bipod, heat bluing near the chamber.
Separate parts: `Body`, `FeedCover` (pivot on its hinge at the rear), `Bipod` (pivot at its hinge, legs
folded), `AmmoBox` (pivot at its mount, drops along -Z), `Trigger` (pivot at its pin). Empty `Muzzle`.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
