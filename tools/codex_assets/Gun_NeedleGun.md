# Gun_NeedleGun (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: Gun_NeedleGun, a mechanoid long-range flechette rifle (colonists can pick it up), ~1.1 m long,
~4 kg. Precise ultratech design: a slender fluted barrel with a magnetic accelerator sleeve (thin orange
emissive line), a narrow angular receiver of black polymer and gunmetal, a transparent magazine window
showing rows of needle darts, a compact optical sensor on top, a skeletal stock. Machined and clean, light
wear on edges, a little dust.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Muzzle` at the barrel tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
