# Gun_ChargeLance (budget 12k tris, 2048 textures + emissive)
Game use: Gun_ChargeLance, a heavy pulse-charged rifle carried by mechanoid lancers (colonists can pick it
up), ~1.2 m long, ~5 kg. Ultratech mechanical design, not human-ergonomic but usable: a long squared
receiver of dark gunmetal and pale grey ceramic plates, a long slim barrel inside an open frame of rails
with charge coils along it (pale blue emissive coils), a boxy power cell behind the grip, a minimal stock
plate, a sensor block instead of iron sights, exposed cabling. Light scratches and dust, the coils' housings
heat-tinted, otherwise precise and machined.
Separate parts: `Body`. Empty `Muzzle` at the barrel tip.
Axis: hand-weapon convention (palm on grip at origin).
