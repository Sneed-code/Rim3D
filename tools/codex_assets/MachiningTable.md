# MachiningTable (budget 10k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: MachiningTable, a workstation for guns, mechanical parts and armour, 3 x 1 cells (2.9 x 0.9 m
footprint), ~0.95 m to the top. A heavy steel workbench with a small metal lathe at one end (chuck, tool post,
tailstock), a bench vise in the middle, a drill press at the other end, a pegboard back panel with wrenches,
files and calipers, a half-assembled rifle receiver on a cloth, a tray of bolts, metal shavings. Machine-grey
paint, oil, worn bright edges, scratched top.
Separate parts: `Body`, `LatheChuck` (pivot on the spindle axis).
Axis: building convention, the front (working side) facing -Y.
