# SculptureGrand (budget 24k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: SculptureGrand, a grand art piece, 2 x 2 m footprint, ~2.8 m tall. A monumental carved stone piece:
a tall spiralling form of intertwined human figures and leaves rising to a figure releasing a bird at the top,
on a round stepped plinth. Warm grey marble-like stone, high polish on the faces and limbs, rough chisel on
the base, fine details. Dust and faint water stains on the plinth.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: building convention, the main figure facing -Y.
