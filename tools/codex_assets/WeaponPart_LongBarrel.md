# WeaponPart_LongBarrel (budget 3k tris, 1024 textures)
Game use: long barrel, a loose replacement rifle barrel weapon part lying on the ground. A heavy fluted rifle barrel ~0.60 m with a threaded muzzle with a thread protector cap, a barrel nut and gas-block at the chamber end, a cloth wrap tied round its middle. Blued steel with a dull oily sheen, darker heat discolouration near the muzzle, light surface rust spots. Bore modelled open at the muzzle.
Separate parts: `Body`.
Axis: small-item convention (origin at bottom centre, the barrel along X).
