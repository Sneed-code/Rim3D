# FloodLight (budget 8k tris, 2048 textures)
Game use: FloodLight, a tall work light that lights a big area, one 1 x 1 m cell, ~2.4 m tall. A weighted
steel base (tripod with sandbags, or a square plate with bolts), a telescoping pole with a clamp, a cross
bar on top with two rectangular LED/halogen lamp heads with cooling fins and wire guards, tilted slightly
down, a power cable running down the pole to a junction box. Yellow safety paint chipped to metal, dusty
lenses. Emissive map for the lamp faces.
Separate parts: `Base`, `Head` (tilt pivot on the cross bar axis). Empty `Light` at the lamp faces' centre.
Axis: building convention, the lamps facing -Y.
