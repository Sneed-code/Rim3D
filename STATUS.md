Long hair that obeys physics (sneed, 2026-11-19; Unreal and a plain C++ solver, `docs/design/hair-physics.md`): long hair clipped through the body because **the old hair swing knew only where the head was, nothing about the back, shoulders, chest or arms**, so any move of the body into the hair went straight through it. Now the long, bob, braid and ponytail styles are **strands that fall under gravity, keep their length, lag behind starts, stops and turns and settle, and are pushed out of capsules round the skull, neck, chest and upper back, shoulders and upper arms** that ride on the bones. The capsules are **fitted to each body** from its skeleton and the hair as styled, so hair at rest stays exactly as made and any body shape works; neighbouring strands keep the sheet of hair together. Full simulation within 25 m of the camera, only pushed out of the body to 50 m, as animated beyond (`Pawns.HairPhysics`, `Pawns.HairDebug 1` draws the capsules). 12 cloud tests (`HairStrandsTests.cpp`). Compiled and checked on the PC (`-CreatorHairDemo`, shadowboxing and sword swings from behind): hair went at most 0.6 cm into the body with physics, against 6.3 cm as animated, and swings with the arms. **Next: sneed judges it in play; then hits and wind pushing hair, and capsules for clothes.**
