Blade into flesh (sneed 2026-11-28): one of the .wav files in this folder plays at random each time a
one-handed sword, a knife, dual blades or a scythe-like blade (the remnant reaper's arms) hits a body
in plain clothes or bare skin. Armour, shields and walls keep their own sounds.

To change them: drop in or delete .wav files here (16-bit PCM WAV, any name). Nothing else to edit.
Which weapons use this folder: data/defs/Things/*.json, "visual": {"sound": {"flesh": "Hit_Blade_Flesh"}}.
