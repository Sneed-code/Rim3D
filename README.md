# Rim3D

A private, single-player 3D colony game built in Unreal Engine 5, closely based on RimWorld and its expansions. You play one colonist in first or third person, or oversee the whole colony from above. The story is our own: a frontier world in the year 6650 ([lore](docs/design/lore.md)).
