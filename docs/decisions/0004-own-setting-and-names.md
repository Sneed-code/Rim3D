# 0004: our own setting and names

Date: 2026-10-25. Replaces the "RimWorld names for now" content rule in `game-design.md`.

## What was decided

sneed: the game keeps RimWorld's mechanics, numbers and premise (slow travel between far-apart
stars, worlds at every level of technology), but the story is our own and may add things RimWorld
doesn't have, magic included. The game stays a personal project; nothing here limits which assets,
models or features we use.

- The setting is written in `docs/design/lore.md`: the year 6650, the fall of the Lattice (the
  Hush), the Fray, resonance (magic) and the peoples and enemies of the setting.
- RimWorld's proper names for the story (peoples, factions, enemies, narrators, places and lore
  words) get our own names, listed in `docs/design/naming.md`. Plain words stay: steel, wood,
  rifle, sniper rifle, wall.
- Made-up item, drug, fabric and animal words (plasteel, survival meal, muffalo...) have proposed
  names in the same file but stay RimWorld's until sneed decides.
- The RimWorld importer applies the "Applied" tables of `naming.md`, so imported data uses our names.
